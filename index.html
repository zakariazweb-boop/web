Welcome to Citadelle Gorges

Genuine Moroccan warmth, wrapped in Amazigh heritage

Nestled in the heart of the Dadès Gorges, just minutes from the spectacular Monkey Fingers, our charming guesthouse offers an authentic and peaceful stay in the Dadès Valley.

Inspired by traditional Moroccan and Amazigh heritage, the property features local architecture, warm earth tones and handcrafted details that create a cosy, timeless atmosphere — an ideal base for a romantic getaway or exploring southern Morocco.
