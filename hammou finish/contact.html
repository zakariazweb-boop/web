How to find us

From the N10 (Ouarzazate–Boumalne Dadès), take the roundabout just before Boumalne Dadès and follow the N704 toward Msemrir. After about 9 km you'll reach our guesthouse in the peaceful village of Aït Ibriren.

Citadelle GorgesGuesthouse · Dadès

A family-run cliffside riad in the Dadès Valley, blending 17th-century Amazigh heritage with warm Moroccan hospitality and panoramic Atlas Mountain views.
